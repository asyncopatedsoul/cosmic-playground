Cosmic Playground

Alien toys that follow real physics. Poke them, stretch them, set them playing on their own, and listen to what happens.

Checking your device…

Enter the playground

Your first tap wakes the sound. Headphones help.

Getting in

  1. Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Press Enter the playground, then click anything. Browsers only allow sound after your first click.
  3. A string and an orrery are already waiting. Click the purple string to pluck it.

Controls

Poke a toyClick it. Shift-click is softer.
StretchScroll the wheel over a toy, or drag its handle (the Spire's glowing cap, an Orrery planet).
TwistQ / E, Alt-drag or right-drag on a toy, or turn a gold peg or the Kettle's rim.
Move a toyDrag its base. Drop it on the chest to put it away.
Make it play itselfClick the toy's pink heart to open its Pulse.
Get more toysOpen the chest at the bottom, or press Tab.
Look aroundDrag empty space. Scroll over empty space to zoom.
ShortcutsSpace pokes the last toy you touched. Esc closes the Pulse.

Tips & caveats

  • Glow effects are on for computers. If the fans spin up or the picture stutters, close other heavy tabs. Sound always comes first, so the picture slows down before the audio does.
  • If you hear nothing, check that the tab isn't muted (right-click the tab) and that the volume slider in the top bar is up.
  • Switching to another tab pauses the animation. Toys keep playing, but some browsers slow background tabs down after a while.

Open it on another device

Scan this with a phone to open this guide there.

Getting in

  1. Open this page in Safari (iPhone, iPad) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap Enter the playground, then tap anything to wake the sound.
  3. iPhone and iPad: if you hear nothing, flip the ring/silent switch to ring (or turn off Silent Mode). Web sound follows it.

Controls

Poke a toyTap it. On screens that sense pressure, a firmer tap is louder.
StretchPinch with two fingers on a toy, or drag its handle.
TwistRotate two fingers on a toy, or turn a gold peg or the Kettle's rim.
Move a toyDrag its base. Drop it on the chest to put it away.
Make it play itselfTap its pink heart. In the ring, tap dots on and off; press and hold a dot for "sometimes".
Get more toysTap the chest at the bottom of the screen.
Look aroundDrag empty space. Pinch empty space to zoom.

Tips & caveats

  • Turn off Low Power Mode / Battery Saver and close other apps for the steadiest sound.
  • Portrait works, but landscape gives the toys more room.
  • Glow effects are switched off on phones to leave power for the sound.
  • If the screen locks or you switch apps, the sound stops. Tap again when you come back.
  • Bluetooth headphones add a little delay between a tap and its sound. Wired headphones or the speaker feel tighter.
Today it's a window, not a world. On Quest 3 the playground currently runs as a flat page in the Meta Quest Browser. Stepping inside in VR, and placing toys on your real table in mixed reality, comes in the next phase.

Getting in

  1. Open the Meta Quest Browser and go to this page. The address must start with https://. Bookmark it so you only type it once.
  2. Point at Enter the playground and pull the trigger (or pinch, with hand tracking).
  3. Make the window bigger: drag its corner to resize it, so the toys are easier to hit.

Controls

Poke a toyPoint and pull the trigger, or pinch with hand tracking.
StretchTrigger-drag a handle (the Spire's glowing cap, an Orrery planet), or push the thumbstick up and down over a toy.
TwistTrigger-drag a gold peg or the Kettle's rim.
Move a toyTrigger-drag its base. Drop it on the chest to put it away.
Make it play itselfPoint at its pink heart and pull the trigger.
Look aroundTrigger-drag empty space. Thumbstick over empty space zooms.

Tips & caveats

  • Hand tracking works, but controllers are more precise for small handles like the harmonic beads.
  • Close other browser tabs. The headset shares one processor between the picture and the sound.
  • Two-finger gestures and keyboard shortcuts aren't available here. Every toy has a handle you can drag instead.
  • A plain address like http://192.168… loads the page but stays silent, because headsets only allow web sound on secure (https) pages. Ask whoever is hosting for the https link.
