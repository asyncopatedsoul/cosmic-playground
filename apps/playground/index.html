Playing

  • Tap a toy to poke it. Shift+Space pokes the last toy softly.
  • Tap a toy's pink heart to set its Pulse, so it plays on its own.
  • Spire: drag the cap to stretch, turn the gold peg to tighten, tap a bead for a harmonic.
  • Orrery: drag planets in and out, drag out of the sun for a new one, drag into the sun to remove one.
  • Kettle: drag across the surface to pour, turn the rim to change the reagents.
  • Move a toy by its base. Drop it on the chest to put it away.
  • Stretch: wheel or pinch. Twist: Q/E, alt-drag or two-finger rotate. Space pokes the last toy.
  • Drag empty space to look around. Tab opens the chest.
  • B shows the aim ray: what a click will hit. Delete puts the aimed toy away, Ctrl+Z brings it back.

Modes

  • Puzzles: the ringed button near the left of the top bar opens the puzzle shelf. Pick a puzzle; each one unlocks the next, and solved ones (✓) can be replayed.
  • In a puzzle, tune the toys until every layer of the Heart in the middle fills. Some controls are locked; the dots on the puzzle button show the layers found. When it blooms the rules lift and the world is yours.
  • ✕ on the shelf leaves the puzzle and goes back to the sandbox. A link can open one directly, e.g. ?puzzle=first-light.
  • Views: the Orbit, Walk and Fly buttons on the HUD switch how you move (see Walking below).
  • ⌂ (the first button on the top bar) goes back to the main menu. Your world is kept: Continue on the menu brings it back, and every saved world is listed there.
  • Constellations (next to it): Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+O opens saved worlds, files and share links. Play together opens a room.

Focus view

  • Shift+click toys to select them (Shift+drag empty ground to lasso, Esc clears). F puts them on a ring in the middle; F or Esc leaves.
  • Tab / Shift+Tab or ‹ › move the lead; 1–9 pick one. The ring holds 8 toys, 12 on a desktop.
  • T opens the Pitch Trace: each toy's notes over the beat, one line per pitch.

Listening

  • M or the speaker mutes everything (the music keeps going); long-press the speaker or press L for the Listen list.
  • Point at a toy: N mutes it for you, Shift+N solos it, H focuses it (hold H to focus only while held). Shift+M clears them all.
  • − / = turn the toy's volume down and up; that volume is part of the music, so everyone in a room hears it.

Walking

  • C walks into the world as an avatar (or use the HUD's walk button); C again goes back to orbit.
  • WASD move, Space jump, Shift sneak. Double-tap Space to fly; Space/Shift go up and down.
  • Click pokes what the crosshair is on; hold on a handle to drag it. Right-click places the hotbar toy, middle-click copies a toy into the hotbar.
  • 1–9 or the wheel pick a hotbar slot; drag chest cards onto it. R carries a toy, V switches first/third person, [ ] stretch.
  • The ⚙ next to the view buttons sets your size and how flying works.
