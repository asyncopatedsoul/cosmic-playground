# Playtest guide: remote multiplayer (itch.io)

You're invited to play **Cosmic Playground** with other testers in different places, at the same time, in one shared world. It takes about **45 minutes**: 5 to get in, 30 to play, 10 for a few questions.

## What you need

- A computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) with a recent **Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari**, or **Windows 10/11** for the app.
- Headphones or speakers. Headphones are better if you're also on a voice call.
- Optional: a game controller. Press any button on it once the game is open.
- Optional: a voice call (Discord, Zoom, a phone) with the other testers. The game has no chat.

You don't need an account.

---

## Before the session (5 minutes, any time)

1. Open `{{ITCH_URL}}` and enter the password.
2. **Browser:** press **Run game**, then the fullscreen button. **Windows app:** install it from the same page with the [itch app](https://itch.io/app), or download the Windows zip, unzip and run `Cosmic Playground.exe` (*More info → Run anyway* if Windows warns you).
3. Click once in the world. You should hear a toy. If not, see *Trouble* below.
4. Open the **people** button at the top and check the **build** at the bottom says `{{BUILD_ID}}`. If it doesn't, reload the page (browser) or restart the itch app so it updates.

The [player guide](USER-GUIDE.md) explains the toys and controls if you want a head start. You don't need it: figuring things out is part of the test.

---

## During the session

1. The host presses **people → Host a room** and shares a six-letter code.
2. Press **people**, type the code, press **Join**. If you see *Waking up the room server…*, wait; it can take a minute the first time.
3. Play for about 30 minutes. Some things to try, in any order:
   - Add a toy from the chest and play it. Can the others hear and see it?
   - Give a toy a Pulse (tap its pink heart) so it plays by itself.
   - Two people stretch the same toy at once. What happens?
   - The host locks the world (people sheet). Try to move a toy. Then unlock.
   - Leave the room and rejoin. Is everything still there?
   - Walk around as an avatar (`C`) and find the other players.
4. If something odd happens, write down the time and what you were doing. Don't stop to report it unless you're stuck.

**Known in this build:** rhythms aren't in perfect step between computers yet, and pokes from other players arrive a moment late. Tell us how much that bothers you, but don't report it as a bug.

## After the session

Reply to `{{FEEDBACK_CONTACT}}` with your answers. Short is fine.

1. Browser or Windows app? Which browser or Windows version? Did you use a controller?
2. How long did it take from opening the email to being in the room? Where did you get stuck?
3. Did the world ever look different on your screen than the others described? When?
4. What did you most enjoy making?
5. What was confusing, or what did you want to do and couldn't?
6. How did the late rhythms and pokes feel: fine, noticeable, or spoiled it?
7. Would you play this again with friends? Why or why not?
8. Anything else, plus the times you wrote down.

If you can, open **people → Details** and copy what it shows into your reply. It tells us your connection quality.

## Trouble

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| No sound | Click once in the world; check the 🔇 button and your volume |
| Stuck on *Waking up…* for over 2 minutes | Reload the page and join again; tell the host |
| "This room runs a different version" | Reload the page (browser) or restart the itch app, then join again |
| Windows won't open the app | *More info → Run anyway*; if Smart App Control blocks it, use the browser |
| Mac or Linux | Use the browser build; desktop apps for those come later |
| Controller does nothing | Press a button on it, then click the game once |

Thank you for testing!
