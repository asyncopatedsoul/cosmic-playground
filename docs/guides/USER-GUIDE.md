# Cosmic Playground: player guide

Cosmic Playground is a playground for sound and shapes. You play with **hypertoys**: strange toys that follow real rules of physics and music. Tap them, stretch them, give them a rhythm, and listen to what they make together. You can play alone, with friends on one computer, or with friends somewhere else.

> This guide covers the **playtest build** (Oct 2026). Some things are rough or missing; that's what the playtest is for. Anything marked *new in this build* is what we most want you to try.

---

## Start

| On | How |
|---|---|
| **Web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) | Open the itch.io page, enter the password, press **Run game**. Use the fullscreen button in the bottom corner for the best view. |
| **Windows app** | Install through the [itch app](https://itch.io/app) (it keeps the game up to date), or download the Windows zip, unzip it anywhere and run `Cosmic Playground.exe`. |

The first tap or click starts the sound. Turn your volume up a little; the toys are gentle.

**Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** The playtest build isn't signed yet. Choose *More info → Run anyway*. Installing through the itch app usually avoids the message. If your PC refuses to run it at all (Smart App Control), play in the browser instead.

## The toys

| Toy | What it is | Try this |
|---|---|---|
| **Monochord Spire** | A string. Longer and looser is lower; shorter and tighter is higher. | Tap the string. Drag the cap to stretch it, turn the gold peg to tighten it, tap a bead for a harmonic. |
| **Kepler Orrery** | Planets around a sun. Farther planets orbit slower. Each one chimes as it passes the line. | Drag planets in and out. Drag out of the sun to launch a new one, drag one into the sun to remove it. |
| **Reaction Kettle** | A chemical pattern that grows and bubbles. Bigger bubbles sound lower. | Drag across the surface to pour, turn the rim to change the mix. |

**Pulse.** Every toy has a pink heart. Tap it to open the toy's **Pulse**: a ring of steps. Tap a dot to turn a step on or off, long-press for "sometimes". Pick a speed with the shapes and press play. Now the toy plays by itself.

**Tuning.** The bar at the top picks the world's scale, so every toy fits together. ✺ *wild* turns the scale off and lets the physics decide.

**Adding and putting away.** Open the **chest** (bottom of the screen, or `Tab`) and drag a toy into the world. To put one away, drag it by its base onto the chest.

## Controls

### Mouse and keyboard

| Do | Mouse | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Poke (play) a toy | Click it (`Shift`+click is softer) | `Space` pokes the last toy you touched |
| Move a toy | Drag its base | — |
| Stretch | Drag a handle, or scroll over the toy | `[` and `]` |
| Twist | `Alt`+drag or right-drag | `Q` and `E` |
| Turn and zoom the camera | Drag empty space; scroll over empty space | — |
| Put a toy away | Drop it on the chest | `Delete` while pointing at it; `Ctrl+Z` brings it back |
| Chest | Click the chest | `Tab` |
| Mute everything / mute or solo a toy | Sound buttons on the toy's badge | `M` / `N` / `Shift+N` |
| Listen closely to one toy | — | `H` (hold for a moment, release to stop) |
| Toy volume | — | `-` and `=` |
| Walk around as an avatar | Mode switch, top right | `C` (then `WASD`, `Space` to jump, double-tap `Space` to fly) |

### Touch

Tap to poke. Drag a toy's base to move it, drag a handle to stretch. Two fingers on a toy: pinch to stretch, rotate to twist. Two fingers on empty space: zoom.

### Gamepad *(new in this build)*

Plug in or pair a controller (Xbox, PlayStation, or most others) and **press any button**. A ring in your colour with your player number appears: that's your cursor. Each controller is a new player.

| Control | Does |
|---|---|
| Left stick | Move your cursor (it snaps onto toys) |
| `A` / ✕ | Poke |
| Hold `A` on a toy's base | Grab it, then slide it with the left stick |
| Hold `A` on a handle | Drag the handle with the left stick |
| `RT` / `LT` (R2 / L2) | Stretch up / down |
| Right stick on a toy | Twist |
| Right stick on empty space | Turn the camera (left/right), zoom (up/down) |
| `X` / □ | Start or stop the toy's Pulse |
| `Y` / △ | Add a toy: `LB`/`RB` pick, `A` place, `B` close |
| Hold `B` / ○ on a toy | Put it away |
| `View` (Create / Share) | Undo your last put-away |
| D-pad | Up: listen closely · Down: mute · Left/right: volume |
| Hold `Menu` (Options) | Leave (your toys stay) |

Editing the steps of a Pulse still needs the mouse or touch.

## Play together

### On one computer

Everyone with a controller presses a button to join. The mouse is always player 1. All players share the camera: whoever moves it last has it.

### With friends somewhere else *(new in this build)*

1. Press the **people** button at the top of the screen, then **Host a room**.
2. You get a **six-letter code**, like `K7QFZM`. Press **Copy invite** and send it to your friends, or read it out.
3. Your friends open the same itch.io page (or the Windows app), press the **people** button, type the code and press **Join**.

Everyone in the room shares the same world: toys, Pulses, tempo and Tuning. Your volume, mute and camera are yours alone.

- **"Waking up the room server…"** The room server sleeps when nobody uses it. The first person to join waits up to a minute. That's normal.
- **"This room runs a different version"** Someone has an older build. Reload the web page, or let the itch app update, then join again. The build id in the people sheet should match for everyone.
- **The host** (first in the room) can **lock the world** so others can play toys but not add, move or remove them, and can hand the host role to someone else.

In this build, rhythms (Pulses) aren't perfectly in step between computers yet: everyone hears the same toys at the same tempo, but each computer starts its rhythms on its own beat. Pokes arrive a moment after they're played.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| No sound | Click or tap once in the world. Check the 🔇 button top right and your device volume. |
| Gamepad does nothing | Press a button on it (browsers only see a controller after a press). Click the game window once so it has focus. |
| The game feels slow | Close other tabs. In the browser, try the Windows app, or the other way round. |
| Can't join a room | Check the code (no `0`, `O`, `1`, `I` or `L` are ever used). Wait a minute if the server is waking up. |
| Saved worlds vanished | In the playtest, worlds saved in the browser are tied to the itch.io page and may not last. Don't keep anything precious there. |

Found a bug? Note the **build id** (people sheet → bottom) and tell us what you did just before. See the playtest guide you were sent for where to send it.
