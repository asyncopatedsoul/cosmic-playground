Cosmic Playground is an all-ages playground for sound and shapes. You play with hypertoys: strange toys that follow real rules of physics and music. Tap them, stretch them, give them a rhythm, and listen to what they make together. Play alone, with a friend on the same computer, or with friends somewhere else.

This is a private playtest build (build {{BUILD_ID}}). Things are rough. Please don't share the password.

Play

  • In your browser: press Run game above, then the fullscreen button. Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
  • Windows app: install with the itch app (it updates itself) or download the Windows zip below. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info → Run anyway.
  • Click once in the world to start the sound.

The toys

  • Monochord Spire: a string. Tap it; drag the cap to stretch, turn the gold peg to tighten, tap a bead for a harmonic.
  • Kepler Orrery: planets that chime as they pass. Drag them in and out; drag out of the sun to launch one.
  • Reaction Kettle: a bubbling chemical pattern. Drag across it to pour; turn the rim to change the mix.

Tap a toy's pink heart to give it a Pulse, a rhythm it plays by itself. The bar at the top picks the scale so every toy fits together. Get more toys from the chest at the bottom; drop a toy on the chest to put it away.

Play together

On one computer

Each player with a controller presses any button and gets a numbered ring in their own colour. Everyone shares the camera.

With friends somewhere else

  1. Press the people button at the top → Host a room.
  2. Press Copy invite and send it, or read out the six-letter code.
  3. Friends open this page (or the Windows app), press people, type the code, press Join.

The room server naps when nobody's using it: the first join can take up to a minute ("Waking up the room server…"). Everyone needs the same build; if the game says "different version", reload this page or let the itch app update.

Known in this build: rhythms aren't perfectly in step between computers yet, and other players' pokes arrive a moment late. Saves made in the browser here may not last.
