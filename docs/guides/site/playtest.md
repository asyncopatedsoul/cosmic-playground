Cosmic Playground is an all-ages playground for sound and shapes. We're running small, invite-only playtests in autumn 2026, and we'd love your help.

## Two ways to take part

| | **Online together** | **Two controllers, one PC** |
|---|---|---|
| Players | 2 to 6, each at home | 2, side by side |
| You need | A browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) or Windows 10/11 | A Windows 10/11 PC and two game controllers (Xbox or PlayStation) |
| Time | About 45 minutes, at a set time | About 40 minutes, whenever suits you |
| What we're testing | Joining a shared world with a room code, and staying in sync | Joining with a button press, and sharing one screen and camera |
| Guide | [Online playtest guide](/learn/playtest-remote/) | [Local playtest guide](/learn/playtest-local/) |

Both use the same private itch.io page. We'll email you the link, the password and the date.

## How to join

Write to **{{FEEDBACK_CONTACT}}** with:

- which playtest you'd like (or both),
- what you'd play on (browser, Windows; which controllers),
- for families: the players' ages (it's built for all ages, and we especially want to watch younger players find their way).

No account, no install needed for the online playtest. Everything runs in the browser; the Windows app is optional.

## What to expect

This is an early build. Some toys are rough, rhythms aren't perfectly in step between computers yet, and the Windows app isn't signed, so Windows asks you to confirm before it runs (*More info → Run anyway*). Your feedback decides what we fix first.

The [player guide](/learn/guide/) explains the toys and controls.

## Privacy

We only ask for your answers to a few questions after the session. The game has no accounts, no chat and no tracking. Saved worlds stay on your device.
